Demo 1 · AI listing check

Post in one sentence. AI checks it before it goes live.

  • Type“Selling 2 OSU student tix $60 each, venmo me first @tickets_usc or text 213-555-0199”
  • AIFills in the game, the price and the private lowest price, and flags the outside payment and contact details, quoting the exact words.
  • RulesA listing with a payment handle, phone number or link can't go live. Fixed rules check too, even if the AI is offline.
There's no chat with strangers on Teezy, so there's nowhere to say “Zelle me first.” The only way to pay is inside Teezy, with the money held.

How it works · Technical & AI

The AI never touches the money.

Student message

“selling osu 70, would take 40”, or a transfer screenshot.

→

Claude (Opus)

  • Reads it into a strict JSON order: event, quantity, price, private limit
  • Flags scam signals with the exact quote
  • Checks transfer screenshots with vision
→

Teezy code decides

  • Matches limits, sets the price
  • Caps at face value
  • Holds and releases money on Stripe
Private by designThe AI only sees one side's limit. Buyer maxes are never shown to sellers.
Defence in depthFixed scam rules run on every message, next to the AI.
Fails safeIf the AI is unreachable, the rules still check posts and trades still work.
Can't be talked into itMessages are treated as data. The AI has no power to change a price or release money.
